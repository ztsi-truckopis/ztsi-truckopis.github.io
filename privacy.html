TruckOpis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the TruckOpis app collects, how it uses Google Drive, and how that information is protected. TruckOpis is the trip, payroll and billing system of ZTSI, used only by ZTSI's own staff.

1. Information TruckOpis handles

  • Business records entered by ZTSI staff: trips, containers, daily trips, customer payments, payroll, expenses, budgets and client details.
  • Staff accounts: name, role and sign-in details for the people who use TruckOpis, and a log of sign-in attempts (time, network address and browser) to protect the system.
  • Attached files uploaded by staff, such as container gate passes and bank deposit slips (JPG, PNG or PDF).

2. How TruckOpis uses Google Drive

Attached files are stored in ZTSI's own Google Drive account. TruckOpis requests only the Google permission "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (the drive.file scope). With it, TruckOpis can:

  • create a TruckOpis folder and the folders under it (for example by year and bill of lading);
  • save the files staff attach, and show or download them again inside TruckOpis;
  • move a file to the Drive trash when staff remove it.

TruckOpis cannot see, read, change or delete any other file in the Google Drive account. It does not use Google data to show ads, does not sell it, and does not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Limited Use of Google user data

TruckOpis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to store and show the files ZTSI's staff attach in TruckOpis, and is not transferred to anyone else except as needed to provide this feature, to comply with the law, or to protect the system.

4. Who can see the information

Only ZTSI staff who sign in to TruckOpis, and only the records their role allows. The files in Google Drive are kept in ZTSI's account and are not shared with anyone. The TruckOpis developer may access the system only to maintain it, on ZTSI's instruction.

5. How it is protected

TruckOpis is used over a secure (HTTPS) connection, staff sign in with their own accounts, and each role sees only what it is allowed to. The Google Drive access key is stored in the server's protected settings, never in the app's code.

6. Keeping and deleting information

Records and files are kept for as long as ZTSI needs them for its business and legal obligations. ZTSI can remove a file at any time (it goes to the Drive trash), and can cut TruckOpis's access to Google Drive at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps & services).

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or about the information TruckOpis holds: ztsi.truckopis@gmail.com.
